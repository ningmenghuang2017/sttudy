📖 What is This?

This is a personal study planner designed to help 3rd graders prepare for the SSAT exam. It helps you organize your study time and track progress.

Create custom study plans, track what you've studied, and see your progress grow. All your plans are saved so you can come back anytime.

🎯 Goal

The goal is to make studying fun and organized so you feel ready and confident on test day!
